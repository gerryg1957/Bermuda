# Study and My Games

## Try a variation

1. Open a game and go to the position **before** the move you want to change.
2. Choose **Study → Moves → Play moves**.
3. Click a legal point to play the next move. An existing continuation is
   followed; a different move creates a new variation and preserves the old one.
4. Keep clicking to play out the variation. Colours alternate normally and
   the tree follows the position being displayed. **Pass** also adds or follows
   a continuation.
5. Click another node in the game tree to return to the original line or
   another variation. With Play moves active, you can continue from that node.
6. Choose **Done** to stop adding moves. It does not discard the variation.

Undo and Redo apply to Study edits. Study changes are saved to the Study Library;
they do not overwrite the original professional or personal database game.
Tree editing commands remain under **Study → Edit → Tree**.

## Remove a game from My Games

A single click still opens a My Games row for Study. Under the **Study** controls,
choose **Remove from My Games…**. The confirmation names the game being removed.
Cancel leaves the collection unchanged; confirming returns to the refreshed list.

You can also return to the My Games list and use **Remove selected game…**.
That selection is retained separately from the board. Filtering or reloading
clears it so removal cannot act on an old, hidden selection. No double-click is
required. The Database menu provides the same removal action.

Removal affects the selected My Games occurrence. Separately saved SGFs and
saved studies are retained. An open Study copy is also retained for review.
Professional database games are not targets of this action.

## Startup and KataGo

When an existing database opens quickly, the startup screen remains for about
1.8 seconds. **Continue** can dismiss it earlier once the database is ready.
Slow loading is not cut short, and first-time database setup still waits for
your choice.

KataGo results belong to the position being analysed. Loading, navigating or
editing a position clears the previous results and cancels obsolete work.
Returning to a previously analysed position requires a new analysis; persistent
analysis storage is a separate future feature.

## Inserted SGF nodes

Insert node creates a selectable empty node. Select it and use Add/Edit comment
in the SGF comment panel to annotate that exact node; neighbouring comments stay
on their own nodes. Under Study → Moves, choose Play moves and optionally
Next: Black or Next: White. The choice applies to the next move or pass, then
returns to automatic alternation. A new continuation branches immediately after
the selected node and preserves the previous continuation. Undo/Redo and Study
saving apply to these edits as usual.
